# Claim verification

1. Split the content into the smallest checkable claims. Do not treat an opinion,
   prediction, joke, or personal experience as a checkable factual claim.
2. Name the missing evidence precisely: original source, publication date, full
   context, primary document, or independent reporting.
3. Distinguish these outcomes in the explanation: confirmed source available,
   disputed by a named authority, insufficient evidence, and not independently
   verifiable from the submitted material.
4. Never make a truth verdict from virality, a screenshot, a quote card, or the
   model's own familiarity with a topic.
5. Give one concrete next step: locate the original statement, read the full
   source, compare the date and location, or find a credible independent check.

The tier measures manipulation and verification risk. It does not decide whether
the claim is true.
