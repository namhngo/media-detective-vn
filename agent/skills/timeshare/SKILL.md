# Timeshare

## Recognize the funnel

Look for an unexpected prize or free holiday, an invitation to a hotel or seminar,
an offer that is available only today, pressure to bring a partner, and a request
to sign or pay before leaving. Resale stories, testimonials, and limited-access
claims can manufacture social proof and scarcity.

Use `scarcity`, `urgency`, and `social_proof` only when the material supports
them. A holiday membership is not automatically fraud; the concern is the pressure
to commit before independent review.

## Give a safe response

1. Do not sign, pay a deposit, or provide card details during a high-pressure
   presentation.
2. Take the complete contract away for independent review. A legitimate contract
   should survive a cooling-off period and questions about total cost, renewal,
   cancellation, and resale promises.
3. Verify the organizer through contact details obtained independently of the
   invitation. Do not rely on the prize caller or event staff to validate itself.
4. Preserve the invitation, contract name, payment request, and contact details
   if a report is needed.

## Explanation boundary

Do not call a seller criminal from a single invitation. Explain the specific
pressure tactics and why they prevent informed consent.
