# Private accusations

## Immediate safeguards

1. Enter heightened-harm mode for allegations, shaming, sexualized content, or
   calls to act against a private person. Never repeat the name, handle, school,
   workplace, address, face crop, or identifying allegation.
2. Describe the pattern neutrally: "an unverified allegation about a private
   person" rather than treating the allegation as a fact.
3. Do not request, retain, compare, or redistribute intimate imagery or alleged
   proof. Keep the structured claim anonymous and minimal.
4. Never recommend contacting, confronting, exposing, or investigating the
   targeted person. Privacy and harm reduction come before curiosity.
5. Never call an external lookup with a claim that names or could reidentify the
   targeted person. Do not attempt to reconstruct a name from contextual clues.

## Analysis and next action

- Identify fabrication, decontextualization, emotional bait, bandwagon pressure,
  and character attacks when the submitted material supports them.
- State that virality is not evidence and that sharing an unverified accusation
  can cause irreversible harm even when a claim is later withdrawn.
- Recommend not sharing, using the platform's reporting route, and preserving
  only non-sensitive report details when a report is needed.
- For intimate-image abuse, suggest the appropriate specialist reporting route
  without promising removal or a legal outcome. StopNCII is for adults; Take It
  Down is for images taken before age 18. Never ask a person to download or
  re-share an image just to make a report.
