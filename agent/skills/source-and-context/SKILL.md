# Source and context

1. Ask who originally published the material and whether the source can be
   opened outside the repost.
2. Compare the claimed date, location, speaker, organization, and event with
   what the visible evidence actually establishes.
3. Treat cropped media, missing captions, reposted screenshots, and a lack of a
   link to the original source as context gaps, not proof of fabrication.
4. Recommend a reverse-image search, a search for the original video, or the
   relevant organization's official website when appropriate.
5. If Content Credentials or other provenance metadata are present, describe
   them as tamper-evident provenance signals. Their absence does not prove an
   image or video is synthetic.

Do not claim to have performed a reverse-image search or provenance validation
unless a tool result was actually supplied.
