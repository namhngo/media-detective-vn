# Health misinformation

1. Do not diagnose, prescribe, or endorse a treatment from a post or screenshot.
2. Flag anecdotes presented as proof, urgency to buy or stop treatment, fake
   expert authority, and claims that discourage professional care.
3. Direct users to a qualified clinician and the relevant public-health or
   medical authority for the specific place and condition.
4. Explain that a personal testimonial, before-and-after image, or product
   review does not establish safety or effectiveness.
5. If the claim suggests immediate danger, recommend urgent local medical help
   rather than additional online checking.
