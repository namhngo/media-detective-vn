# Civic claims

1. Keep neutral language and analyze the evidence, not the political position.
2. Separate a factual assertion from opinion, advocacy, or prediction.
3. Prefer the original law, official notice, public record, transcript, or
   documented statement over a screenshot or quote card.
4. Check the issuing organization, publication date, document number, and full
   context before describing a claim as supported or disputed.
5. Do not invent official sources or treat an AI summary as a primary document.
