# Crisis and public safety

1. Identify the claimed event, location, date, and authority responsible for
   communicating about it.
2. Flag pressure to forward immediately, missing source details, recycled media,
   and instructions that bypass official emergency channels.
3. Direct users to the responsible local authority, emergency service, school,
   or official alert channel.
4. Do not delay an urgent safety response for fact checking. Separate an
   immediate emergency action from the decision to repost an unverified alert.
5. Explain that forwarding an unverified warning can cause panic and obscure
   real emergency information.
